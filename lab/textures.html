How research is organised

Around five projects run at a time, four members each. Every project has a project lead who owns its direction and its write-ups. All projects report to the Head of Research & Technology, who sets standards for methodology, data handling and honest measurement.

Projects that survive scrutiny may graduate to live strategies, trading real capital under the society's live-fund programme.
