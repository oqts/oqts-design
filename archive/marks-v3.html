OQTS · vesica refinements · v0.3 · answering "it only says OQ"
V1 — The lens
Reframe: the circles are not letters. Two domains — price and value, market and model — and the gilt lens where they intersect is where trading happens. The tail keeps the Q for those who want to read it; the meaning no longer depends on it.
V2 — One construction, four letters
The direct answer to "it only says OQ": every letter of OQTS is derivable from the same two-circle construction. O is a circle; Q is a circle with a radial; T is the common tangent with the lens axis; S is the ogee traced through the lens with half-radius arcs. The mark stays the vesica — this strip is the argument, and it becomes brand collateral (tote, membership card, about page) rather than the logo itself.

V3 — The settled seal
The vesica above the closing double rule — the ledger mark for "total, settled". Composition reads as an institution's seal, not as initials, which also answers the OQ worry. Strong for letterheads, stamps, and the site footer.
V4 — The chain
Four circles, one per letter, each passing through its neighbour's centre — the vesica relation propagated. The literal answer to "four letters", at the cost of simplicity; needs the two-circle mark as its small-size stand-in.
